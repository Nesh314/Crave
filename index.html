📱 Add CRAVE to your iPhone Home Screen

  1. Open this website in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep the name CRAVE Journal, then tap Add.

The CRAVE icon will then appear beside your other apps. Open it from the Home Screen like an app.

Notifications: this version stores your chosen reminder time, but iPhone web apps have platform-specific notification requirements. For a dependable daily alert, you can also create a repeating reminder in Apple Reminders.
